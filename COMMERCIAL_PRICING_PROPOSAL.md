**Proposal Status:** For Review & Approval  
**Implementation Date:** November 2026

---

## 1. Objective

The objective is to establish a structured subscription model for LFD Cloud that supports different categories of manufacturing customers while generating sustainable recurring revenue.

LFD Cloud offers an integrated 15-Step Lean Transformation Framework, combining software-enabled modules, AI-powered analysis, manufacturing planning, and consulting capabilities.

The proposed subscription structure consists of four plans:

1. **Free**
2. **Small Business**
3. **Mid-Size Business**
4. **Enterprise**

The pricing model is designed to encourage customers to start with essential Lean Transformation capabilities and upgrade as their operational requirements grow.

## 4. 15-Step Lean Transformation Framework – Access Strategy

The subscription model distinguishes between software-enabled capabilities and consulting-supported capabilities:

| Category | Free | Small Business | Mid-Size | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic platform access | Limited | Included | Included | Included |
| Core software-enabled workflow (11 steps) | Limited | Included | Included | Included |
| Advanced AI capabilities | Limited | Standard | Advanced | Custom |
| Project management | Basic | Standard | Advanced | Advanced |
| Team collaboration | — | Limited | Included | Included |
| Additional consulting-supported steps (4 steps) | — | — | Optional consulting | Included as agreed |
| Multi-plant management | — | — | — | Included |

**Important Note:** Small Business and Mid-Size plans both provide access to the core 11 software-enabled steps. The distinction is based on usage capacity, active projects, AI allowances, collaboration, and centralized governance. The remaining four steps are offered through Enterprise consulting and implementation scope.

### 6.1 7-Day Free Trial Policy

| Plan | Price | Access |
|---|---:|---|
| **Free Trial** | **$0** | **7 days** |

**7-Day Free Trial for LFD Cloud.**

#### After 7 days:
- **Trial expires automatically:** Access concludes seamlessly at the end of 7 days without auto-conversion.
- **Customer receives expiry reminders:** Automated notification emails keep customer teams informed before expiration.
- **Customer chooses a paid subscription:** Customer evaluates their experience and selects an appropriate paid tier (Small Business, Mid-Size, or Enterprise).
- **No automatic payment without authorization:** No payment method is charged without explicit customer authorization; zero surprise billings.
- **Project data retained for 30 days under the proposed retention policy:** Factory layouts, PPQ analyses, and transformation maps remain safely stored for 30 days, enabling easy reactivation upon subscription.
